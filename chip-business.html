Grade 10 Personal Project

Crunch Co.

A personal passion project that grew into something real - making handcrafted chips from scratch and sharing them with my community.

Overview

What is Crunch Co.?

Crunch Co. was born out of my Grade 10 Personal Project - a year-long initiative that challenged students to pursue something they were genuinely passionate about. I chose to make chips. Specifically, I wanted to take something I loved and actually create it from scratch, sharing it with the people around me.

Over three months, I developed original chip flavours, designed my own packaging, and shared my creations with classmates, teachers, and neighbours throughout my community.

The Story

Where the Idea Came From

I've always been drawn to the idea of creating something from nothing. When our Personal Project supervisor asked us to find a topic we could dedicate months to, making something with my own hands felt like the obvious answer. I didn't want to plan something in theory - I wanted to actually do it.

I chose chips because they combined two things I love: food and creativity. There's a surprising amount of craft involved in getting a flavour profile just right, and I was excited by the challenge of getting a flavour just right, and the joy of watching someone try something I made and genuinely love it.

The name "Crunch Co." came from a late-night brainstorming session with my family. Simple, memorable, a little playful - exactly the vibe I was going for.

What I Did

Making It Happen

I started by researching food safety guidelines for home production, making sure everything I made was safe to share. From there it was all about experimenting: testing flavour combinations over several weeks, involving friends and family as taste-testers, until I had three recipes I was genuinely proud of.

I designed the logo and packaging myself, going through many iterations based on feedback. I set up a small table at school events and spread the word through friends - it was all very grassroots, which made it feel even more personal.

Keeping track of ingredients, costs, and how many bags I had ready each week taught me a lot about planning and follow-through. Staying organised while also just enjoying the process was a balance I had to figure out as I went.

Impact

Reflection

What I Learned

This project taught me that seeing something through takes more grit than you expect. There were batches that didn't turn out right, slow weeks that tested my patience, and moments where I had to figure things out as I went. But each stumble made me more determined, not less.

I also learned to really listen. Some of the flavours people loved most came from feedback I almost dismissed. That taught me to stay curious, stay open, and trust the people around me - qualities I'll carry with me always.

Most of all, Crunch Co. gave me the confidence to know that I can take an idea from nothing and make it real. That feeling is something I want to keep chasing.
