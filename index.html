Supplies for Students

After noticing that many students in my community lacked basic school supplies, I organized a city-wide donation drive. I reached out to local businesses, coordinated with school administrators, and mobilized volunteers to collect and distribute hundreds of items to students in need — ensuring no child had to go without the tools to learn.
